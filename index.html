Trip operating rules

1 · Protect bookings

Skytree, TeamLab, Nozomi 357, Sagano and Hozugawa override optional shopping/sightseeing.

2 · Fuji exit rule

Preferred Yamanakako departure 09:30–09:45. 10:00–10:15 is the hard late edge, not the target.

3 · ASICS is non-negotiable

Sep 27 camera browsing may compress; ASICS keeps its 75–90 minute fitting block.

4 · Cluster shopping

Fujiya + Map Camera on one day; ASICS + optional Onitsuka in Shinjuku; easy chains later.

5 · Walking is real time

Major station changes and cross-neighborhood walks include buffers rather than assuming platform-to-platform travel only.

6 · Oct 4 stays conservative

The booked Osaka→Tokyo Shinkansen anchors the day; keep the Tokyo-to-Haneda transfer buffer protected for the 16:30 flight.
